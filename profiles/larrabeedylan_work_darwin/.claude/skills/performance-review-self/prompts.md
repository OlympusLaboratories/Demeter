# Performance Review Prompts

<!-- Paste the review prompts/questions you need to answer below -->


1. What top 2 accomplishments are you most proud of in Q1 and Q2 & why? (up to 100 words) *
Choose what you're genuinely proud of, which may not be the most visible thing your manager saw. The "why" matters: explain the impact, the difficulty, or what you learned. This is also a chance to surface wins your manager might have missed.

2. What is one developmental goal/area you need to work on in order to have an increased impact going forward? (up to 75 words) *
Be candid and name a real growth area and tie it to the impact you want to have.
(context, I could be better about communicating what I'm working on to others even if they are not directly working with me on it)
